# Literature search protocol

> Fill in BEFORE searching, so the search is reproducible and cannot be tuned to the conclusion. The literature-scout records each executed search in `research/literature/search-log.md`.

## Research questions for the review
(What exactly must the literature answer? Link to charter items.)

## Sources
(arXiv, DBLP, ACM DL, IEEE Xplore, Semantic Scholar, OpenReview, venue proceedings, Google Scholar. List the ones used.)

## Search strings
(Exact strings per source, plus synonyms and the keywords from the charter.)

## Inclusion criteria
(Years, venues, peer-review status, topic, task and setting.)

## Exclusion criteria
(Why a paper is dropped. Each exclusion is logged with the reason.)

## Snowballing
(Backward and forward citation chasing from key papers: how many hops.)

## Contradicting evidence
(How papers that contradict the charter hypotheses are searched for deliberately.)

## Quality and recency
(How peer-reviewed vs preprint is marked, how retractions/newer versions are checked, search date.)
