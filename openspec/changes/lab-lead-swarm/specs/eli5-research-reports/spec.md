# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Specifies the structure, plain-language and academic-jargon translation requirements, citation verification rules, and mechanical validation for producible research reports (`research/reports/<topic>.md`) generated by the Lab Lead for the user.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Five-Part ELI5 and Jargon-Translated Research Report Structure
The system SHALL require every research report in `research/reports/<topic>.md` to include five mandatory sections (`## 1. Mental Model`, `## 2. What Has Already Been Done` with a `### Jargon Decoder` table, `## 3. Where the Holes Are`, `## 4. Original Research Ideas & DIY Kill-Tests`, and `## 5. Active Learning Check-In`), certainty labels (`VERIFIED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`), and cite-checked literature references.

#### Scenario: Validating a complete research report
- **WHEN** `scripts/lab report-check research/reports/<topic>.md` is executed on a report containing all five sections, a diagram block, a Jargon Decoder table, resolved DOIs/arXiv IDs, and certainty labels (`VERIFIED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`)
- **THEN** the CLI exits 0 and logs a passing `report_check` event in `research/logs/audit.jsonl`

#### Scenario: Rejecting a report with missing sections or unverified citations
- **WHEN** `scripts/lab report-check research/reports/<topic>.md` is executed on a report missing any of the five required sections, missing the `### Jargon Decoder` table, or containing unverified citations not marked `UNVERIFIED`
- **THEN** the CLI exits non-zero and lists each missing section or citation defect

### Requirement: Jargon Synchronization with Glossary
The system SHALL require terms introduced in a report's `### Jargon Decoder` table to be recorded in `research/glossary.md` with their plain-English meaning and analogy.

#### Scenario: Checking glossary synchronization
- **WHEN** `scripts/lab report-check research/reports/<topic>.md` runs
- **THEN** it checks that decoded terms in `research/reports/<topic>.md` appear in `research/glossary.md` and reports a defect if any decoded term is missing from `research/glossary.md`
